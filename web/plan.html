2 Set what the aircraft needs

Length of straight run

Longer at altitude and in heat. Water bodies that are too short drop out.

Clear width of water

How wide the open water must be along that run. 100 m is close to the published minimum. A drawn-down reservoir can stay long but become too narrow, so try a wider setting for reservoirs in a dry year.
