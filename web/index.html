Five steps, from a blank map to a decision.

    A longer run at altitude or in heat.

  3. See where loads are slow

    Minutes per load for every point on the map.

  4. Test a dry year

    Put each reservoir at its lowest measured month.

  5. Decide

    Where to send other aircraft. Which reservoirs to protect. Where a water point is needed.
