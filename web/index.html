01 · The problem

A scooper refills by skimming a lake. It needs about a nautical mile of straight, open water. In a drought that water shrinks, in the same weeks the fires come.

How to read the percentages

A map draws a reservoir at the size it is when full. From each satellite image, Skimline measures how much of that full-size shape is still water. 92% means nearly full. 42% means more than half the surface has turned into dry bank, the pale rim in the second image. Less surface means shallower water and a shorter straight run for an aircraft.

Skimline measures this reservoir from every clear satellite pass.
