Where can a water bomber refill, and how long does each load take?

Scooper aircraft skim a lake or reservoir to refill. They need a long, straight stretch of open water. This map measures that stretch for every water body, from open data.

        What planners can use this for

        • Find where scoopers are slow. Red ground that has also burned needs other aircraft or a water point.
        • See which reservoirs matter in a dry year. The ones that lose their run, and how much ground depends on them.
        • Brief crews from elsewhere. One method across regions and borders.
